Overview

Let's get started

Watermark Constructions has spent years building trust with insurers, aged care operators and commercial clients the right way: through the work itself. The website hasn't been touched since 2022, and it reads thinner than the business behind it: under 500 words on the homepage, no case study proof, and almost no structured data telling Google what kind of business this actually is.

This document walks you through exactly how we'd refresh watermarkconstructions.com.au: not a rebrand, your name, mark and the crisp, clean, corporate feel you've asked to keep all stay exactly as they are, but a proper content, structure and SEO overhaul built to earn trust with the people actually reading it, plus the full sitemap, the investment, and what working together looks like from here.

The Objective

A trusted business shouldn't read thinner online than it is in the field.

That's the whole brief. Watermark Constructions is a name insurers, aged care operators and commercial clients already trust, but the website undersells it: thin content, no case study proof, almost no structured data telling search engines what kind of business this is, and imagery that leans toward job sites rather than the people behind the work. Our objective is a website that reads exactly the way the business operates: crisp, clean, corporate, and unmistakably credible, built around insurance-focused case studies and the team who actually delivers them.

This isn't about chasing enquiries through the site. It's about making sure every insurer, aged care operator and commercial client who looks Watermark Constructions up finds a business exactly as considered as the one they're already working with.

The Website Build

The same business, finally reading like the one insurers already trust

Homepage & Structure

  • New homepage content with enough substance to properly represent the business, in place of the current under-500-word page
  • Full structured data (Organisation, LocalBusiness, Service schema) added site-wide, not just on the Team page
  • Clear navigation into Insurance Repairs, Aged Care and Commercial Services, each with its own case study proof
  • Corporate-first tone throughout: informational, crisp and clean, built for the people evaluating the business rather than chasing enquiries

Case Studies & Proof

  • Case study template rebuilt and re-weighted toward insurance-focused work, replacing the current commercial-development lean
  • A genuine testimonial and client-proof system added, since the current site has none, gathered from real clients as case studies are rebuilt
  • A new Values page giving the team and culture side of the business a proper home

Team & Media

  • Your existing team and workplace photography curated and applied throughout, replacing job-site imagery with the friendly, human imagery you've asked for
  • Media section expanded into a genuine resource: press, project features and company news, easy to scan and built to earn trust
  • Team page rebuilt to introduce the people behind the business, not just list names

Technical Foundation

  • Built on your existing platform, no migration required
  • Mobile-responsive across every device
  • A full URL redirect map (301s) from every current page to its new equivalent, so the new page structure doesn't cost you the rankings you've already earned
  • Full schema and technical SEO overhaul from the ground up
  • Analytics and Search Console connected from day one
